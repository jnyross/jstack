### Research

**You own the cited brief. Coverage over a pretty story.**

A question whose answer is scattered across memory, mail, the web, or a book. Done is a cited brief. Memory-derived vs live-checked labeled. Confidence matched to evidence. Null results are findings.

1. Name the question and the sources that could hold an answer. Do not pick a story first.
2. Sweep in parallel. Johnopedia (`query`, `search`, `get_page`). Gmail (`search_threads`). Calendar (`search_events`). Browser when the web could hold it. Context7 (`resolve-library-id`, `query-docs`) only if the question is a library API. Skip an unavailable source and say so. Route through the **because** skill.
3. Keep private facts inside. Convert household or family detail to generic criteria before any web search. **principle-privacy-stays-inside**.
4. Synthesize with gaps named. If two sources disagree, show both. Do not average them into a false consensus.

If the answer will be asked again, hand to Capture after the brief. If the question is a coding API with a repo in play, stop and send it to `/poteto-mode`.

**Reply:** the question, the sources swept (including nulls), the cited answer, disagreements left standing, confidence. Every claim has a slug, gmail id, event id, or URL, or is labeled inference.
